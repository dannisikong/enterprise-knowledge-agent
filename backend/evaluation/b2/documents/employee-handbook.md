# Employee Handbook

## Employment and probation
New employees complete a 90-calendar-day probation period. Managers provide role reviews at day 30 and day 75. An unsuccessful review may lead to an extension or termination in accordance with applicable law.

## Leave and benefits
Employees receive 10 days of paid annual leave per calendar year after completing probation. Requests should be submitted in the HR portal at least 5 working days before leave begins. Managers approve requests based on team coverage.

For unexpected illness, employees should notify their manager and HR as soon as practical on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required for illness-related absence lasting more than 2 consecutive working days.

Eligible full-time employees may enroll in the company health plan within 30 days of their start date.

## Conduct
Employees must protect confidential company information and use company systems only for authorized business purposes. Suspected policy violations should be reported to HR or Security.
