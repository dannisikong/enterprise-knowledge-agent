# Travel and Expense Policy

## Approval and booking
Business travel requires written approval from the department head before booking. Requests include destination, dates, purpose, and estimated cost. Use the company travel portal. Flights under 6 hours should be economy class. Flights of 6 hours or more may be premium economy with department-head approval. Business class requires executive approval regardless of duration.

## Reimbursement
Expense claims must be submitted through the finance portal within 15 calendar days after the trip ends. Itemized receipts are required for individual expenses above RMB 200. Claims include business purpose, date, amount, and cost center.

## Meals and transport
Reasonable meal expenses are reimbursable up to RMB 150 per person per day while traveling. Local transport should use public transit or standard ride-hailing. Premium vehicle categories require prior written approval.

Exceptions require written approval from the Finance Director before the expense is incurred.
