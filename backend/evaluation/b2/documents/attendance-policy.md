# Attendance and Working Hours Policy

## Standard schedule
Standard hours are 09:00–18:00, Monday through Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Teams may agree different schedules with their department head.

## Flexible arrival and core hours
Employees may start between 08:00 and 10:00, complete their agreed daily hours, and coordinate schedules with their manager. Core collaboration hours are 10:00–16:00.

## Lateness and absence
An employee expecting to arrive more than 15 minutes late must notify their manager before the scheduled start time. Unplanned absence must be reported to the manager and HR as soon as practical.

## Overtime and remote work
Overtime must be approved in writing by the manager before it is worked. Approved overtime should be recorded within two working days. Remote work requires manager approval in advance. Remote employees must remain reachable during core collaboration hours and follow the data-security policy.
