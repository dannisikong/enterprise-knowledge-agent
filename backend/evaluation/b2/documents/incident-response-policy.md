# Incident Response Policy

## Severity and response targets
P1 incidents are critical service outages or confirmed major data exposure. The on-call incident lead must acknowledge a P1 within 15 minutes, start coordination immediately, and update stakeholders at least every 30 minutes.

P2 incidents are major degradation affecting multiple users. The on-call team must acknowledge a P2 within 1 hour and update stakeholders at least every 2 hours.

P3 incidents are limited-impact defects or service degradation. The responsible team must acknowledge a P3 within 4 business hours. The target for a workaround or mitigation is 2 business days.

## Reporting and evidence
Employees must report suspected security incidents immediately through the security hotline or incident portal. Reports should include observed time, affected systems, symptoms, and actions already taken. Do not investigate suspected malware independently or delete evidence.

The incident lead coordinates evidence preservation. Logs, relevant messages, and affected system snapshots must be retained as instructed.

## Communications
Only the incident lead or designated communications owner may issue external statements about an incident. Employees must not speculate publicly or share details with unauthorized parties.
