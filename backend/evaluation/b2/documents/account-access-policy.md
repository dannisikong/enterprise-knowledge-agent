# Account and Access Management Policy

## Provisioning
A manager submits an access request through the IT service desk for each new employee. It must identify the employee, role, required systems, and business justification. IT provisions access after manager approval and identity verification.

## Authentication and credentials
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for company email, source-code hosting, cloud consoles, and VPN access. Users must not share MFA codes or approve unexpected authentication prompts.

Passwords must be unique and stored only in the approved password manager. Credentials, API keys, and recovery codes must never be placed in source code, tickets visible to unauthorized users, or shared documents.

## Reviews and termination
System owners review privileged access quarterly. The manager must notify IT before an employee's final working day. IT disables accounts and revokes active sessions no later than the employee's departure time.

## Lost devices
Users must contact the IT service desk immediately if a company device is lost or credentials may have been exposed. IT may suspend accounts during investigation.
