# Data Security and Information Classification Policy

## Classification
Company information is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Restricted data includes authentication secrets, personal data at scale, and unreleased customer datasets. Data owners assign classifications and review them annually.

## Handling
Confidential and Restricted data may be accessed only by authorized personnel with a documented business need. Restricted data must be encrypted in transit and at rest. Confidential or Restricted data must not be copied to personal devices or unapproved cloud storage.

## External sharing
External sharing of Confidential data requires written approval from the data owner and an approved contractual or legal basis. Restricted data must not be shared externally unless the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) gives explicit written authorization.

## Retention and disposal
Records follow the approved retention schedule. Authorized electronic disposal requires secure erasure; physical records must be cross-shredded. Suspected loss or unauthorized disclosure must be reported immediately under the Incident Response Policy.
